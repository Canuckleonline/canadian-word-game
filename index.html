Canuckle: guess the Canadian word in six tries

Moose, maple, toque or hoser? One five-letter word with a Canadian twist every day, plus unlimited practice rounds. For the past-puzzle calendar, daily hints and more word games, play Canuckle on the main site.

Any five letters with at least one vowel are accepted as a guess.

How to play

Five letters, six guesses, one Canadian word

Type any five-letter guess and press Enter. After each guess the tiles change colour to show how close you are:

MAPLE

M is in the word and in the right spot.

SKATE

A is in the word but in a different spot.

BISON

O is not in the word at all.

Good to know

  • A new daily word appears at midnight in your local time.
  • Practice mode gives you a fresh word whenever you want.
  • Stuck? The hint button reveals the topic, the number of vowels and the first letter.
  • The high-contrast button switches to orange and blue tiles.
  • Your streak and stats stay in your own browser.

Strategy

Tips to solve it in fewer guesses

  1. Open with vowels. Words like CANOE (four vowels) or AUDIO quickly show which vowels you can rule out.
  2. Think Canadian. Once you know two or three letters, run through the topics – is it an animal, a food or a hockey term?
  3. Don’t forget repeats. Answers like MOOSE and GOOSE use the same letter twice.
  4. Use grey letters. The keyboard greys out letters that are not in the word, so you never waste a guess on them.
  5. Save hints for the end. The first letter is the strongest clue – keep it for guess five.

Mini glossary

Canadian slang you may need

Toque
A knitted winter hat. Also spelled “tuque”.
Hoser
A friendly insult for a clumsy or unsophisticated person.
Loonie
The one-dollar coin, named after the loon on its back.
Toonie
The two-dollar coin – a mix of “two” and “loonie”.
Double-double
A coffee with two creams and two sugars.
Keener
Someone who is extremely eager, often at school.
Eh
Added to the end of a sentence to invite agreement.
Two-four
A case of 24 beers.

FAQ

Canuckle questions

How do I play Canuckle?

Type a five-letter word and press Enter. Each tile changes colour to show whether the letter is in the right spot, in the word but in another spot, or not in the word at all. You have six tries to find the Canadian word.

When does a new word appear?

A new daily word appears every day at midnight in your local time zone. You can play as many extra rounds as you like in practice mode.

What counts as a Canadian word?

Every answer is linked to Canada in some way – wildlife, food, places, hockey and curling, Indigenous culture, history or Canadian slang. French-Canadian words are written without accents.

How do hints work?

Tap the hint button to reveal up to three clues one at a time: the topic of the word, how many vowels it contains, and its first letter.

Is my progress saved?

Your daily progress and statistics are stored in your own browser only. Clearing your browser data resets them.

Is Canuckle the same as Wordle?

The rules are similar to Wordle, but every Canuckle answer has a Canadian theme and each round ends with a short fun fact about the word.
